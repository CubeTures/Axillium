## Feature Set

- Graduation
    - A system to guide someone along their journey
        - Initiation
            - Base information
                - (Vague) Location
                - Addiction (preset based on location) => become leader if not applicable
            - Anonymous
                - Low barrier to entry
                - Simple forms to fill everyday
                - Able to view other's conversations and blogs
            - Categorization of users into groups
                - Local to their area
                - Similar addictions
            - People in this tier as long as they need
                - Remove the shame 
        - Apprentice
            - Start speaking in the chat
            - Semi-anonymous
            - More complex forms
            - Have some responsibility that keeps you coming back
            - Paired with sponsor who looks out for you and checks in (opt-in)
                - Unlocks panic button
                    - Redirects to other sponsors in the group in case yours is not available
        - Sponsor
            - Sponsor other members of the group
                - Help yourself by helping others
                - You keep what you have by giving it away
        - Leader
            - A leader is the starter of a group
            - Become the leader of a group
                - Lead discussions
            - Clears sponsored users of being able to be a sponsor (clears them as clean)
            - Set responsibilities for users
        - Influencer
            - Make public blog posts that inspire others
        - Graduated
            - No longer using the app
